Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Video Uploader is a private tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels. It runs on the owner's own computer.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission (Google sign-in), the app uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • upload videos and thumbnails to the owner's YouTube channel, and
  • read the name of that channel, to confirm the right channel is connected.

It does not read, collect or change any other data in the Google account.

Where data is kept

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with or sent to anyone else. The app has no other users.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
